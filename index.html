Springline Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026 · App: Springline (Android, package com.sickmind.springline; iPhone and iPad, bundle ID com.sickmind.springline.ios) · Developer: Sickmind

This policy explains what information the Springline game ("the app") collects, why, and the choices you have. Springline has no user accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your game progress (completed levels, stars, coins, hints, garden, unlocked skins, achievements) and settings (sound, music, haptics, notifications, language) are saved in the app's private storage on your device. A short local log of game events is also kept on the device. We do not receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Local notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your device (for example, when a garden plant has grown). These are created on the device and do not send data to us. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your device's settings (Android or iOS).

4. Advertising and your choices

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a Google consent form is shown before personalised ads are used. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices in the game.
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • On iPhone and iPad, the app does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency), so it cannot access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). You can also turn off personalised ads from Apple in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • Some US states give you the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising; you can use the options above or contact us.

5. In-app purchases

The app currently has no in-app purchases. If purchases are added later, payments will be handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we will not receive your payment details, and this policy will be updated.

6. How we use and share information

We use the information above to run and improve the game, fix bugs, show ads that fund the game, and send optional messages. We do not sell personal information for money. Information is shared only with the service providers listed above, or when required by law.

7. Data retention and deletion

On-device data stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Data held by Google services is kept according to their retention settings (for example, Firebase Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months; Crashlytics crash reports for up to 90 days). To ask us to delete data linked to your installation, email us (see below) and we will help.

8. Security

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we use only well-established providers.

9. Children

Springline is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to processing or withdraw consent. Contact us to exercise these rights. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes (for example, if new features or services are added). The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
